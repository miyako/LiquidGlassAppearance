Translucency without an alpha channel
The form style sheet accepts six-digit hex and nothing else.
So the blend is computed before the value is written:
C = Cs x a
+ Cb x (1 − a)
Cs the tint
Cb the ground behind
a the rung's alpha
Worked example - a floating group over the dark canvas:
tint (white)
canvas
a = 0.032
result
#1E1E1E
#FFFFFF
the measured rung
#252525
The tint flips with the scheme; the alpha does not. That is why a group reads DARKER
than its pane in light mode and LIGHTER in dark mode, from one stored number.
